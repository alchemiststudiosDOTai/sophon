**Steps**:

3. Map `CliProvider::Brave` to `ProviderId::Brave`.
4. Map `CliProvider::Exa` to `ProviderId::Exa`.
5. Keep `src/bootstrap/provider_registry.rs` free of `crate::cli` imports.
